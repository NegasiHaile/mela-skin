# 2026-08-27 11:01 EAT — Second pass: darker palette, full-bleed hero, about & skincare

Follows `2026-08-27-0950-meeting-implementation.md`. That pass got the pricing
decision, the brand package and the structure in. This one fixes four things it
got wrong or did not go far enough on, all of them re-read off the transcript.

## 1. The palette is not dark enough

**What the meeting said.** Aser [00:35:00]: *"like this palette that I just
shared — you want it to be that darker chocolate, right, the second, the second
brown."* Abseret: *"Yeah, I just would like it to be as cool as that brown. Do
you understand what I mean by that, Nagasi? Like that second brown. Do you have
the image up in front of you?"* … *"Like more of a coffee color, more like cooler
than how warm that is. If it's going to be too dark for a website, you can alter
the color, but I just would like it to be a little bit more cool, not so red."*
And when shown the darker option: *"Yeah, the bottom one is a bit better."* Then:
*"And if you are going to use a lighter color, you can use creams."*

**What the last pass did.** Moved the signature flood from `#74370c` to
`#602F0F` — the *third* colour on the sheet. That is cooler and less red, but it
is not "the second brown", and the site still reads mid-brown rather than dark
chocolate.

**The fix.** The flooded sections take Primary 2, `#2C190B`. That frees Primary
3 for the panel role and moves cocoa onto Secondary 3:

| Token | Was (this morning) | Now | Palette | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--color-ms-espresso` | `#160F09` | `#160F09` | Primary 1 | the deepest ground, and body text on light at 70–80% |
| `--color-ms-field` | `#602F0F` | **`#2C190B`** | **Primary 2** | **the signature flood — the second brown** |
| `--color-ms-panel` | `#421E04` | **`#602F0F`** | Primary 3 | the lighter tint over a dark ground: dropdown hover, media plates |
| `--color-ms-cocoa` | `#2C190B` | **`#421E04`** | Secondary 3 | headings on the light grounds |

Everything else is unchanged. Nothing gets worse and most things get better,
because a darker ground raises the contrast of everything reversed out of it:

| | on the old field `#602F0F` | on the new field `#2C190B` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cream `#F4E7D6` | 9.03:1 | **13.79:1** |
| ivory / shell `#FDFCF8` | 10.71:1 | **16.35:1** |
| sand `#CBAA7D` | 5.02:1 | **7.66:1** |
| gold `#dcbc63` | 5.97:1 | **9.13:1** |

Headings on light: cocoa `#421E04` reads 14.41 / 13.41 / 12.15:1 on shell,
paper and cream. Every pairing the site uses still clears AA and the numbers go
back into the `globals.css` comments.

Also retuned for the new grounds: `PatternField`'s field / espresso / panel
tones, the two flooded booking bands' radial gradients, `viewport.themeColor`,
the web-app manifest, and `FIELD` in `scripts/build-brand-assets.py` (so the
favicons and the social card follow).

## 2. The hero is still doing too much

**What the meeting said.** Mo [00:47:02]: *"my concern with the draft is that
it's too busy. Even when I look at it, I'm not sure what exactly to look at …
it would be best if we can do it in a clean format that feels like elegance and
excellence."* Abseret [00:56:08]: *"I want our brand to be more clean. I don't
want it to be so busy."* And on the reference sites, [00:47:02] Mo again: *"I
liked the clinic background"*; Abseret [00:48:09]: *"I also like the Elevate one
where they had the clinic name."*

Negasi on the call [00:57:11]: *"even if we are having the background image we
can minimize the contents that we display on the first screen."* That is the
brief, in the meeting's own words, and it is what this implements.

**The first screen is now exactly four things:**

```
Richer. Radiant. You.

Medical and cosmetic dermatology built for melanin-rich skin,
on one record and under one roof.

[ Book a consultation ]  [ Treatment menu ]
```

**Off it come:** the large gold brandmark (it is already in the header lockup
four centimetres above, so it was the name twice), the tracked
"DERMATOLOGY & COSMETIC CLINIC · WESTLANDS, NAIROBI" eyebrow (also in the header
lockup), and the framed stack beside the type with its caption and dots.

**The background becomes one full-bleed image**, behind everything, with a
brand-coloured scrim over it. This is the Canadian reference's move and the
frame it gives the Elevate one.

**What the image is: a licensed stock photograph, credited on the page.** There
are still no photographs of the clinic — Aser [00:49:00]: *"one thing is, we
don't have the clean space pictures yet."* So the reception frame carries
[unsplash.com/photos/pt0nGH-NvoA](https://unsplash.com/photos/pt0nGH-NvoA)
(Aalo Lens, Unsplash License, free for commercial use), cover-cropped to
2400×1350 as `public/images/hero-clinic-sample.webp`.

It was chosen for what is in it rather than for being available: a wood-slatted
feature wall, pendant lights over a reception desk, warm marble, and **no other
business's signage in frame**. That last one ruled out most of the candidates —
the first Pexels result for "clinic reception" has another clinic's logo and name
on the wall, which would have put a competitor's brand on Mela Skin's hero. It
is also close to what the clinic is actually building: Aser, 01:01:07, on the
renovation — *"I think we need to have some of the wood panels tested to see how
it looks"* — and Abseret, 00:33:33, on what the landing photograph should be:
*"the desk where people are going to sit, with the two lit hanging lights."*

**It says it is a sample.** `Sample image · Unsplash / Aalo Lens` sits bottom
right in 11px cream. The Unsplash License requires no attribution; the line is
there so the photograph cannot pass for the clinic's own room, which is the
thing Abseret objected to at 00:17:24 (*"I do want it to be real"*). Any frame
marked `sample` prints its credit, so the line removes itself when a real
photograph replaces it.

**The generated brand ground is still there**, as the floor: if every frame in
`heroFrames` is emptied it renders the `4_Pattern` motif at architectural scale
over Primary 2. It is the one hero ground that needs nobody's permission.

**What happens when the photographs arrive.** `heroFrames` in
`constants/brand.ts` keeps the three-slot structure — reception & entrance,
then cosmetic, then medical — and the background cross-fades through whichever
of them has a `src`. With one it is a still; with three it becomes the sequence
Mo asked for (*"it shows the entrance, the reception, the waiting area and then
flips into the cosmetic procedures … and then the next, medical procedure"*)
with no code change. Filling in a path and dropping the `sample`/`credit` fields
is the whole job.

### The hero opens on the same brown as every other route

The first version of this scrim was `ms-espresso` over a heavily vignetted
ground, and it made the home page open a stop darker than the band at the top of
`/about`, `/skincare` and the rest, all of which are `bg-ms-field` flat.

All three scrim layers are `ms-field` now — a flat 60% wash over the whole frame,
a left weight to 62% under the type column, and a bottom lift under the buttons.
Measured off the render rather than modelled:

| | measured ground | contrast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole hero, home | `#4B3828` | — |
| Whole hero, `/about` | `#4C3A2C` | 1–4 of 255 apart, i.e. the same brown |
| Behind the tagline (1440px) | `#7A6B5E` | ivory 5.0:1 — AAA at that size |
| Behind the paragraph | `#554332` | cream 6.6:1 |
| Behind the tagline (390px) | `#675546` | ivory 6.9:1 |
| Behind the credit line | `#493622` | cream/70 5.2:1 |

The credit started as `ms-sand/55` and reached only 2.7:1 on the lightest part of
the scrimmed photograph, which fails AA even for large text. It is cream at 70%.

## 3. The about page buries the two things it is for

**What the meeting said.** Abseret [00:16:05–00:17:24], setting out the
structure: *"usually you'll have a little bit more of an about section about
each provider. So either about our clinic, our mission and vision, and then each
provider that we have a little bit of a bio about them."*

So the page is **about the clinic**, then **the providers** — and the providers
are meant to be seen, not read about. Currently the clinician block sits fifth,
after the story, the six principles and the seven assessment readings, and its
portrait is an empty slot.

**The fix:**

- **Reorder.** Story (about the clinic) → **Our providers** → How we work →
  Skin assessment → The premises. The on-this-page nav follows.
- **Two providers, portrait and bio side by side**, alternating sides, which is
  what the existing layout already does once there is more than one entry:
  **Dr. Margaret Gachanja** (Nairobi) and **Dr. Abseret Hailu** (Toronto,
  consulting — *"I will be basically consulting and collaborating with Aser on
  this project"*, 00:02:15).
- **Sample portraits go back in.** The last pass replaced the generated portrait
  with an empty slot, which is defensible but leaves the page unreviewable. A
  sample is in place now and labelled as one in `constants/placeholders.ts`, so
  it is obvious it is a stand-in and a one-line swap.

Both providers' bios, registration numbers and special interests stay bracketed
until they send them. Dr. Gachanja's qualifications are the ones she stated on
the call; Dr. Hailu's are hers to supply.

**What those pages actually do:** a collection. A clean grid of large square

No product is invented. Each tile carries its real category and a bracketed name
until the ranges are chosen, and renders the brand panel with the treatment mark
set large — the finish this site already uses for a photograph it is waiting on.

## As built

Written after the work. All four items above went in. The list below is where
the finished thing differs from the plan, plus what got checked.

### Deltas

- **The token shuffle needed a fourth move, not three.** Putting Primary 2 on
  `ms-field` freed Primary 3, which went to `ms-panel`; that in turn pushed
  `ms-cocoa` off Primary 2 onto Secondary 3. Four tokens, four official values,
  no duplicates, and the four dark browns on the sheet are now each doing one
  job: espresso the deepest ground, field the flood, panel the lift over a dark
  ground, cocoa the heading colour on light.
- **The generated ground needed two rounds of tuning.** The first attempt paired
  a 0.62 vignette with a 0.80 scrim in the component, and between them they
  flattened `#2C190B` to near-black — the darker palette was there and invisible.
  Vignette down to 0.42, pattern alpha up to 0.42, scrim down to 0.55 → 0.25 →
  transparent. The scrim exists for the photograph that is coming; cream already
  reads 13.8:1 on the ground unassisted.
- **`HeroFrames.tsx` is gone, replaced by `HeroBackground.tsx`.** Not renamed:
  a captioned framed stack beside the type and a full-bleed cross-fading ground
  share no markup. The caption, the tab-list dots and the pause-on-hover all
  went with it, because none of them belong on a background.
- **The sample portrait is labelled on the page, not only in the source.** A
  generated face standing in for a named doctor is exactly what Abseret objected
  to, so `photos.gachanja.caption` renders "[Sample image. Not Dr. Gachanja.]"
  under the picture on both `/about` and the home teaser. The first attempt put
  that only in the label, where the page never showed it.
- **The four-category shelf became the eight-tile collection**, replacing it
  rather than sitting alongside it. Two grids of the same content on one page is
  the busyness this whole pass is against.
- **The skincare page's title and lede changed** — it opened "The four things
  that actually hold", which was the routine's headline on a page that now leads
  with the collection.
- **The home teaser's copy and target moved with the second provider**: "One
  clinician, one record" → "Two clinicians", and its button now goes to
  `/about#clinician` rather than the top of the page.
- **Dr. Hailu's registration placeholder was shortened.** At full length it
  wrapped the role line onto two lines; the jurisdiction question moved into her
  credential list where it has room.

## Third revision, same day: the hero photograph and its colour

Two corrections after review of the pass above.

### The background had to be a real photograph

The generated brand ground read as a flat field rather than as the clinic
space the reference sites put behind their type. So the reception frame now
carries a licensed stock photograph, and the generated ground has dropped to
being the fallback. Details are in section 2, rewritten in place.

**How the photograph was chosen.** Four candidates were downloaded and compared
before this one:

| Candidate | Rejected because |
| --- | --- |
| Pexels 33812025, "modern medical reception" | Another clinic's logo and name are on the wall. It would have put a competitor's brand on Mela Skin's hero. |
| Pexels 7031704, spa zone | Mosaic tile and a moss wall. Nothing to do with this palette. |
| Pexels 6758532, hotel lobby | Grey marble. Cold, and the wrong colour family. |
| Pexels 34297951 | People in surgical scrubs and masks. |

The one used is Unsplash `pt0nGH-NvoA`: wood-slatted feature wall, pendant
lights over a reception desk, warm marble, nobody in frame, no signage. It is
also the closest of the four to what the clinic is actually building.

**No signage in frame was a hard requirement**, not a preference. It is the one
thing that would make a sample photograph actively harmful rather than merely
provisional.

### The hero colour had to match the other routes

It opened a stop darker than `/about`, `/skincare` and the rest, all of which are
`bg-ms-field` flat. The cause was two `ms-espresso` scrims over a heavily
vignetted ground — three separate darkenings stacked.

Fixed by making every scrim layer `ms-field`, which puts the whole site on one
opening colour. Measured off the render: home `#4B3828`, `/about` `#4C3A2C`.

One thing this surfaced: the credit line started as `ms-sand/55` and reached only
2.7:1 on the lightest part of the scrimmed photograph, which fails AA even for
large text. It is `ms-cream/70`, at 5.2:1.

### Also fixed

- **A corrupted `.next` build.** Three manifests (`export-marker.json`,
  `functions-config-manifest.json`, `pages-manifest.json`) had been left
  truncated by overlapping `next build` and `next start` runs during review, and
  the server was throwing `SyntaxError: Unexpected token ' '` on every request.
  `rm -rf .next` and one clean build. Nothing in `src/` was involved. Worth
  knowing: do not run a build while a `next start` is serving the same `.next`.
